# Product photos

Put one photo per product in this folder, named after the product slug:

    images/<slug>.jpg

For example:

    images/dior-sauvage-edt.jpg
    images/ysl-libre.jpg
    images/baccarat-rouge-540.jpg

The slugs are listed in `js/products.js`. Until a photo exists, the site shows a
sand-coloured "PRODUCT PHOTO" placeholder, so you can add photos one at a time.

Tips: use JPG, roughly square or 4:5 portrait, around 1000 px on the long side
and under 300 KB for fast loading on mobile. Only use photos you own or have
permission to use.
